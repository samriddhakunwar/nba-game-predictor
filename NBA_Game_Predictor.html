Method

How it was built

  1. CollectTeam box scores for regular-season games, 2010-11 to 2025-26. NBA.com data covers seasons through 2023-24, and ESPN covers the two newest. Both sources were checked against each other on 2023-24.
  2. EngineerPossessions, offensive and defensive ratings, and Dean Oliver's Four Factors for each game.
  3. Look back onlyEvery feature uses games before the one being predicted. Early-season numbers lean on last season until about 10 games have been played.
  4. Rate teamsElo ratings are updated after every game with a margin-of-victory multiplier. Each summer they are pulled 25% back toward average.
  5. ModelLogistic regression and gradient boosting on home-minus-away differences, with settings tuned on .
  6. Test is scored once at the end, and walk-forward validation checks seasons.

What the model can't see

  • Injuries, rest days and lineup news, which move odds the most on game day
  • Trades during the season, until they show up in results
  • Betting market lines, which already combine all of the above
  • Playoff games, which are excluded here

Ways to extend it

  • Add player-level availability from the box-score files to weight each team by who actually played
  • Compare against closing betting lines as the benchmark
  • Predict the point margin as well as the winner
  • Add playoff and play-in games, which follow different patterns
